A little clarity

Good questions.
Simple answers.

You stay in control of your photos and every deletion.

Is Sift really free?

Yes. Sift has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, ads, or cleanup limits. You do not need an account.

Why does Sift need Photos access?

Sift needs permission to show the photos and videos you want to review. You can grant access to selected items or your whole library. To change that choice later, open Settings → Apps → Sift → Photos. With limited access, Sift shows only the items you selected.

Does swiping immediately delete a photo?

No. Let go adds an item to your review queue. It stays in your library until you open Review, confirm the selection, and approve the system Photos request. You can undo staged decisions or keep an item from the review screen.

Can I recover something after deleting it?

Recovery happens in the Photos app, in Recently Deleted. Apple normally keeps deleted items there for 30 days. If iCloud Photos is enabled, confirmed deletions also apply to your other synced devices. In-app undo applies before deletion.

Do my photos leave my device?

Sift does not upload your photos or your review decisions to an app-operated server. Your sorting progress stays in the app's local storage. Opening an iCloud-only item may cause Apple's Photos framework to download it through your iCloud account. Read the privacy policy for the full details.

Why are some items missing or unable to load?

Check your Photos permission first. Limited access shows only selected items, and Favorites are protected by default. You can choose whether to include Favorites in Sift's settings. Media stored only in iCloud may need an internet connection before its full preview can load.

What happens when I reset my progress?

Resetting progress clears saved sorting decisions. It retains the recorded deletion total and the identifiers used to avoid counting the same deletion twice. Resetting progress does not restore photos that have already been deleted.
