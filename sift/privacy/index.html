Sift Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Sift helps you review and organize your iPhone or iPad photo library. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or app-operated servers.

Photo access

Sift accesses photos and videos only through Apple's Photos framework and only with the access you grant in iOS. You can grant access to selected items or your library, change the selection, or revoke permission in Settings. The app reads the available media and metadata, such as dates, media types, favorites, and album membership, to display cleanup collections and previews.

Sift does not upload your photos or videos to us. When you open media stored in iCloud, Apple's Photos framework may download it through your iCloud account. Apple's services and your device settings govern those transfers.

Information stored on your device

Sift stores review decisions, photo-library identifiers, identifiers of items successfully deleted through the app, deletion totals, and app preferences in its local app sandbox. This lets it resume your progress and count distinct deleted items without counting repeated confirmations twice. An item restored through Photos can be reviewed again; deleting that same identifier again does not increase the recorded total. Sift does not create a separate permanent copy of your photo library.

Sift does not provide its own cloud synchronization. iOS may include app data in system-managed backups according to your settings.

Your controls

Swiping an item only records a decision. Sift requests deletion through Photos after you review the selection and confirm the request. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple may synchronize confirmed deletions across your devices. Recovery after deletion is handled in Apple's Photos app.

Resetting progress clears saved review decisions while retaining the recorded deletion total and identifiers used to avoid duplicate counting. If a progress file is unreadable, an explicit reset preserves a local copy before starting fresh. Deleting Sift removes its local app data; system-managed backups remain subject to your device and account settings.

Sharing

Sift does not sell personal information or send your review decisions, photo identifiers, or usage events to an app-operated service or third-party analytics provider.
